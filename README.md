# ShadowMap

**See your digital footprint. Take control of your privacy.**

ShadowMap is an educational cybersecurity platform that helps people understand how pieces of information shared online can connect to reveal a digital footprint.

## 🎯 Project Mission

To empower students and young people to understand digital footprints, recognize potential privacy risks, and make informed decisions about their online presence.

## 🚀 Planned Features

- Interactive digital footprint visualization
- Fictional identity demonstrations
- User-controlled profile builder
- What-If privacy simulator
- Privacy risk explanations
- Digital privacy learning resources
- Interactive knowledge challenges

## 🛠️ Technology Roadmap

- React
- TypeScript
- React Flow
- Python
- FastAPI
- NetworkX

## 🔐 Privacy Principles

- Fictional data by default
- No unauthorized personal-data collection
- No social media scraping
- Transparent relationship visualization
- User control over submitted information

Early development — MVP planning and setup.
